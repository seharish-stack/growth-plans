1 · The ads built followers, not buyers

The Ad Library shows a big push between October 2025 and March 2026 (around 620 ads matched "Ben & Hollie Academy"). Almost all of them asked people to follow the page or DM a word like SIXTY, READY or Airbnb. That grows an audience and a DM inbox, but it never gives a cold viewer a reason to commit an hour of their attention. All of those ads are now switched off.

2 · It's a big decision with one short step before the call

Rent-to-rent asks people to believe four things before they'll pay: it's legal, landlords will say yes, the numbers work, and they can do it alongside a job. Your FAQ answers exactly these, which tells me they come up on calls. A short VSL can't move someone through all four, so you're probably doing a lot of the convincing on the call itself.

3 · Everyone who doesn't book is lost

The VSL page goes from video to booking form. There's no email capture, so anyone who watches and isn't ready today (most people, for a decision this size) leaves without you being able to follow up. Every one of those is a warm lead you've already paid or worked for.

4 · The growth depends on the two of you posting

8.6k followers and 204 posts is a solid, engaged base, but it means new calls come in roughly in line with how much you post and reply in DMs. The moment you take a week off (or move countries), the calendar feels it.

You don't have a proof problem or an offer problem. You have a "where do warm-but-not-ready people go?" problem. A live masterclass answers it.

03 · The plan in one picture

Add one step: a live masterclass, fed by ads, that books the calls for you

Keep everything you have. Point paid traffic at a masterclass instead of a follow, capture every email, and let the class do the convincing so your calls are with people who already believe it works.

My pick: a £17 paid live workshop

For a model where "do I need money to start?" is a real question, a small price is a useful filter. People who pay £17 turn up far more often than people who registered for free, they arrive more committed, and the ticket sales plus an order bump can cover a big share of the ad spend. Your calls get fewer tyre-kickers.

The free version, if you'd rather

A free class gets more registrations for the same budget and suits a wider audience. Show-up rates are lower, so it needs stronger reminders. My suggestion: launch paid, and test a free version for two weeks once the paid one is running, then keep whichever books more qualified calls per pound spent.

04 · The masterclass

"Your First Airbnb Without Owning Property"

A live 75-minute workshop with both of you, weekly at first (say Wednesday 7pm UK), then run from a recording on a schedule once it's dialled in. Every segment answers one of the four beliefs people need before they'll get on a call.

  1. How rent-to-rent actually works (and why it's legal)The landlord gets guaranteed rent and no voids, you run it as serviced accommodation. Your Channel 4 story is the perfect open: "the property on TV? We don't own it." (10 min)
  2. Choosing a location that booksHow you pick an area, what demand looks like, and why some areas are saturated and others aren't. Share your screen and show one real area check. (12 min)
  3. The landlord conversation, liveBen plays the landlord, Hollie pitches. Show the exact words that get a yes, and how to handle "is this sublet?" This is the bit people will remember. (15 min)
  4. One real deal, every number on screenRent, set-up costs, cleaning, platform fees, nightly rate, occupancy, profit. You already post deal breakdowns, so this is a longer version. It answers "do the numbers work?" (13 min)
  5. Doing it alongside a jobRav and Mohini on 12-hour shifts, Lisa leaving corporate in six months, Helen and Dave at 20 minutes a day. Short clips or screenshots, then the 60-day plan. (10 min)
  6. The invitation and live Q&AExplain the mentorship (unlimited 1-1, WhatsApp, in your corner until the first property) and invite anyone serious to take the 60-second qualifier and book a call. Then answer questions live. (15 min)

Who sees them
  • Cold, UK: 25 to 50, interests around Airbnb hosting, property investing, side income and financial freedom. Broad targeting with strong creative usually wins here.
  • Warm: everyone who engaged with your Instagram in the last 365 days, watched 50% of a reel, or visited the VSL page. Usually the cheapest seats you'll ever buy.
  • Lookalikes: built from your past students and booked calls once the pixel has data.
How to run it
  • Start at around £100 a day: one campaign for cold, one for warm.
  • Optimise for the purchase (the £17 seat), not for link clicks or follows.
  • Launch 6 to 9 ads, kill the weakest weekly, and make two new ones every week.
  • Scale the winners by about 20% every few days while cost per seat holds.

07 · Follow-up

The emails and messages that make people turn up and book

Most of the money in a masterclass is made in the follow-up. These run automatically from your email tool and WhatsApp or SMS.

Before the class: 4 emails + 2 messages
  • Straight away: "You're in" with the link, calendar buttons and your VSL as homework.
  • Day before: "The deal we'll break down tomorrow" (a teaser of the numbers).
  • Morning of: Hollie's short story: "The landlord who said no three times."
  • 1 hour before + 10 minutes before: short reminder email and WhatsApp/SMS with the link.
After the class: 5 emails over 7 days
  • Replay is up (48 hours), with timestamps and the qualifier link.
  • The four objections answered one by one: legal, landlords, money, time.
  • A student story each day for three days (Lisa, Anna, Rav and Mohini).
  • Replay closing: last chance, with a clear "book your call" button.
No-shows: 2 emails + 1 message

"We missed you, here's the replay", then a short WhatsApp from Ben: "Did you get a chance to watch the deal breakdown? Any questions?" Personal, one line, no pressure.

Before every sales call: show-up sequence
  • Confirmation with a 3-minute "what we'll cover on the call" video from Ben.
  • Reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before, by email and WhatsApp.
  • A short "case study to watch before we speak" so they arrive already sold on the model.

09 · Quick wins this week

Five things you could do before building anything

  1. Add an email capture to the VSL pageEven just "Send me the training and the deal breakdown". Start building the list today.
  2. Check your Meta pixelMake sure it fires on the VSL page, the qualifier and the booking confirmation, so the ads have data to learn from.
  3. Retarget VSL viewersA small always-on budget (£10 to £20 a day) showing student wins to people who watched but didn't book.
  4. Ask for Trustpilot reviews8 reviews undersells 100+ people helped. A personal ask to your last 30 mentees could triple it in a fortnight, and it makes every ad work harder.
  5. Turn the winter ads back on, with a new endingYour best performers already exist. Re-cut the last five seconds to point at the VSL page while the masterclass is being built.
