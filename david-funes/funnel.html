Meet David

From rock bottom to an epic comeback

At 26, David had his first full-blown manic episode and his whole world came crashing down. From Bible college to a mental hospital, from buying a nightclub to being homeless, he lived a stranger-than-fiction story.

Then he pulled off a comeback against all odds. He wrote it all down in his memoir, Manic in Miami, and now shares his story with more than 178,000 people across Instagram and TikTok so nobody feels alone.

Today he coaches people rebuilding after a major setback, whether that's a diagnosis, a breakup, a lost career or a personal crisis.
