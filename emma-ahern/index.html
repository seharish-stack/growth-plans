Who it’s for

Because every person is remarkably unique

  • Parents and carers looking for new ways to understand and share feelings at home.
  • Teachers, SENCOs and teaching assistants who want neuroaffirming approaches in the classroom.
  • Speech and language therapists, OTs and other clinicians curious about the ECM.
  • Anyone ready to look beyond deficit-based thinking, towards something more honest and more human.

Here’s what you’ll learn

Five things we’ll cover in one hour

Why language can be a barrier, not a bridge

The idea behind the Social-Language Barrier of Emotional Communication, explained in plain language.

Interoception, explained

What it is, and why noticing internal body signals matters when it comes to expressing emotions.

Double Empathy in everyday life

Why communication goes both ways, and what that changes for the adults in the room.

A first look at the ECM

How the Emotion Communication Map supports the communication of emotions beyond the limitations of language.

Communication Passports in practice

How a simple passport helps families, schools and services share what works for each person.
