Meet Julie

A therapist who has walked this road herself

Dr. Julie de Azevedo is a licensed clinical social worker with a PhD and more than 30 years of experience counselling women, couples and families. She founded Wasatch Family Therapy and is the author of The Assertiveness Guide for Women.

She is also a divorcee after 36 years of marriage, a mom and a grandma, still sorting through messages from her own conservative religious background.

Today she coaches women worldwide through faith transitions, mixed-faith marriages and mid-life reinvention.
