Why you're leaving money on the table

50 to 1

Your $5,000 offer now earns about $100 a lead

Base44's public affiliate rate is a flat $100 per paid sign-up. It takes 50 sign-ups to equal one sale of the program you used to charge $5,000 for.

Every lead

Your team calls everyone, buyer or not

Free attracts browsers. The only filter is a $20 to $50 a month question, so your callers spend their day on women who were never going to invest.

5,500+

Trained women with nothing to buy next

With 120+ free lessons, most people take months working through them alone before they'd ever ask about 1:1. There's no offer waiting when they're ready.

1 partner

Your income depends on Base44's terms

If Base44 changes its payout or ends the partnership, the whole funnel stops earning overnight. None of the revenue is your own offer.
